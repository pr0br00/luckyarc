FAQ

Can I lose my deposit?

No. Your USDC principal is tracked separately from the prize and is withdrawable in full at any time. Only the amount above principal (yield and sponsor top-ups) is ever paid out as a prize.

Where does the prize come from?

Deposits sit in an ERC-4626 vault. Whatever the vault earns above the sum of user deposits, plus anything sponsors add via fundPrize, is the prize.

How is the winner chosen?

Weighted by deposit size — 100 USDC has 10× the odds of 10 USDC. The draw is two transactions: one pins a future block, the next reads that block's hash as the seed. On Arc PREVRANDAO is always 0, so single-transaction randomness would be grindable; the two-step design closes that.

Who can trigger the draw?

Anyone, once the 24h timer passes. A keeper does it automatically, but the button is yours too.

Is there a token?

Not yet. See the LUCKY section above — and treat anything claiming otherwise as a scam.

Is this audited?

No. It is open source with a test suite, and all limitations are listed in the README. Deposit only what you are comfortable with on new infrastructure.

Honest limitations: the commit-reveal draw is better than same-block entropy but is not a VRF (the randomness source is swappable for one when Arc gets it). Winner selection is O(n). ERC-4626 rounding can cost a wei or two. Details in the README.
